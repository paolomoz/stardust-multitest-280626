# PRODUCT — Sony Group Portal (current state, descriptive)

_Register: **brand** (corporate marketing/IR portal). Provenance: inferred from landing copy + brand surface._

## Users
- Investors, analysts, and shareholders (Investor Relations).
- Press, journalists, and partners (News Releases, corporate facts).
- Prospective employees / candidates (Careers).
- General public and Sony enthusiasts following products, technology, design, sustainability.

## Product Purpose
The Sony Group corporate portal — the umbrella hub for Sony Group Corporation. It is
**not** a commerce site; it presents the group's businesses & products, corporate
information, technology programs, sustainability (CSR) initiatives, design philosophy,
careers, and investor relations. Each section is an editorial/landing surface that
links out to deeper sub-sites.

## Brand Personality
- Minimalist, precise, premium, confident.
- Photography- and story-led; lets imagery and product carry the page.
- Monochrome chrome (black/white) with generous whitespace; restraint over decoration.
- Corporate but culture-forward (films, music, audio, entertainment tie-ins).

## Anti-references
- Cluttered corporate intranets; dense IR data tables as a homepage.
- Loud gradients, heavy shadows, rounded "friendly" UI.
- Stock-photo blandness — Sony uses real product/film/event imagery.

## Design Principles
1. Photography first — full-bleed hero imagery and a rotating story carousel lead.
2. Monochrome restraint — black header, white canvas, grey hairlines; color comes from photography.
3. SST typography — Sony's proprietary corporate typeface sets the entire voice.
4. Editorial grid — category-tagged news cards, clean section landings.
5. Quiet motion — auto-rotating hero carousel and subtle reveals, never gimmicky.

## Content surfaces (templates)
- **Home** — hero carousel + Creative Entertainment Vision + Latest News rail + social row.
- **Section landing** — Products, About Sony Group, Technology, Design, Sustainability (overview pages linking deeper).
- **Listing** — News Releases / Press, IR News (dated item lists).
- **Article / editorial** — Message from the CEO, Purpose & Values, Tech/Design stories.
- **Static / legal** — Privacy, Cookie, Terms, Accessibility, About this Site, Site Map.
