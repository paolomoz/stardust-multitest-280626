# PRODUCT — Starbucks (current state)

**Register:** brand (marketing / consumer landing site fronting an ordering app)

## Users
Coffee drinkers and Starbucks Rewards members in the US who come to browse the menu, find a store, order ahead, manage gift cards, and join the loyalty program. Secondary: job seekers, investors, business partners, press.

## Product Purpose
The public marketing surface for Starbucks Coffee Company. It showcases seasonal products, the full beverage and food menu with nutrition, the Rewards loyalty program, gift cards, and store/ordering options, and routes deeper actions into the authenticated app/account flows.

## Brand Personality
Warm, optimistic, premium-but-approachable. Coffee-craft heritage paired with a friendly, contemporary voice ("It's a great day for coffee"). Signature Starbucks green and the Siren mark anchor a calm, generous, photography-led layout.

## Anti-references
Cluttered fast-food menu boards; cold corporate SaaS dashboards; discount/coupon-led promo design.

## Design Principles (observed)
- Photography-led: real product and lifestyle imagery carries every page.
- Generous whitespace, large rounded product cards, pill-shaped CTAs.
- Single dominant brand green for primary actions; cream/deep-green editorial bands for warmth.
- Clear hierarchy: one big hero, then categorized card grids.
