# PayPal (US) — Current State

## Register
**brand** — marketing/landing surface for paypal.com (the `/us` consumer +
business marketing site, not the logged-in product app). Large display
headlines, photographic hero cards, pill CTAs, stat counters, trust/security
messaging. Distinct from the authenticated wallet product.

## Users
- **Consumers** — people who pay, send money, shop, get cashback rewards,
  use buy-now-pay-later (Pay in 4 / Pay Monthly), and manage a digital wallet.
- **Businesses / merchants** — sellers who accept payments (online checkout,
  invoicing, virtual terminal, QR code, POS hardware), and enterprises seeking
  payment platforms.
- **Help-seekers** — existing users looking up how-to / FAQ articles and
  contacting support.

## Product Purpose
PayPal is a global digital payments platform. The marketing site exists to
acquire and educate two audiences: consumers (the PayPal app, debit/credit
cards, rewards, BNPL, security) and businesses (accept payments, POS, business
resources). It routes visitors to sign-up, app download, product detail, the
Business Resource Center (editorial), pricing/fees, and self-service help.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, modern, friendly-but-secure. Big confident typography, generous
whitespace, bright optimistic blues against navy, real lifestyle photography
(people, shopping, everyday moments), animated stat counters and motion. Tone
is plain-spoken and reassuring; safety/privacy is a recurring pillar.

## Anti-references
- Not a dense fintech dashboard; not jargon-heavy.
- Not a dark, edgy crypto aesthetic.
- Avoids legalese in primary copy (pushes terms to footnotes/legal hub).

## Design Principles (observed)
- Photography-led heroes and layered photo cards over flat illustration.
- Pill-shaped CTAs, high-contrast blue-on-white and white-on-navy.
- Trust signals (security, buyer protection) woven throughout.
- Modular sections: hero → feature trio → BNPL → rewards → security → send/receive.
- Business vs Personal toggle as the top-level IA split.
