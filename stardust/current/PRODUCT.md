# PRODUCT — Bank of America (current state, descriptive)

_provenance: extracted from https://www.bankofamerica.com/ (6 pages, Playwright)._

## Register
**brand** — a large consumer-banking marketing surface. Pages sell and explain
products (checking, savings/CDs, credit cards, home/auto loans, investing) and
route authenticated users to Online Banking. Marketing voice over tool voice.

## Users
- Consumer banking customers and prospects (checking, savings, cards, loans).
- Existing customers logging in to Online Banking (login panel is on the home hero).
- Small business and wealth/Merrill audiences (segmented via top utility nav).
- People researching financial topics and security/fraud guidance.

## Product purpose
Help people manage their money and choose financial products, and provide a
trusted, secure entry point to online/mobile banking. Brand purpose line:
"What would you like the power to do? … help make financial lives better through
the power of every connection."

## Brand personality
Trustworthy, established, reassuring, civic. Big institution that signals safety
(FDIC trust strip, security center, fraud guidance) while staying approachable
and benefit-led ("6% cash back", "$200 online bonus offer"). Patriotic palette
(navy + red + white flagscape).

## Anti-references
- No hype/startup tone, no playful slang. No dark patterns in disclosure.
- Not a sparse fintech aesthetic — content-dense, many product entry points.

## Design principles (observed)
- Lead with the customer benefit (rate, bonus, "no annual fee") in big numerals.
- Real photography of people + product (credit-card renditions, lifestyle).
- Persistent trust signals (FDIC band, security links, equal-housing).
- Card-grid information architecture; everything is a "choose your option" surface.
- Clear, underlined link CTAs and pill primary buttons.

## Templates observed
1. **home** — utility+main nav, login panel, credit-card showcase, content modules.
2. **product-landing** — cash-back-credit-cards, compare-credit-cards (offer grid, card comparison).
3. **section-overview** — auto-loans, deposits (intro + product cards + tools/FAQ).
4. **editorial/security** — avoid-bank-scams (hero image + "Trending scams" card grids + tips).
