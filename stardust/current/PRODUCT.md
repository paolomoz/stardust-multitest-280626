# PRODUCT — Xfinity (current state)

**Register:** brand (marketing/landing site for a consumer telecom provider)

## Product purpose
Xfinity (Comcast) is the consumer brand for home internet, mobile, TV/streaming,
and home security. The site's job is **acquisition and self-service**: present
plans and promotional offers, route visitors into the address-based plan builder
and checkout, and support existing customers (bill pay, move service, account,
support hub). Editorial "hub" and "learn" content drives SEO and buyer education.

## Users
- _provenance: inferred from copy + IA._ Prospective residential customers shopping
  internet/mobile/TV bundles and deals; existing Xfinity members managing service,
  paying bills, or moving; deal-seekers comparing Xfinity vs AT&T/Verizon/T-Mobile;
  local-market shoppers checking availability by city.

## Brand personality
Confident, friendly, value-forward, and fast-talking. Copy leads with the benefit
and immediately backs it with a price proof ("Or get 300 Mbps WiFi, $40/mo for 1
year"; "Cut your mobile bill in half. Plus, try free for a year."). Punchy short
declaratives, plain language, heavy promotional cadence. Visually bold: black
full-bleed heroes, large headlines, rounded image hover-tiles, the signature
Xfinity violet→magenta accent.

## Anti-references
- _provenance: inferred._ Not minimalist/luxury, not enterprise-austere, not
  text-heavy. Avoids quiet/understated; the site is dense with offers and motion.

## Design principles
1. **Benefit + price, immediately.** Every offer tile pairs a bold claim with a
   concrete number and a single CTA.
2. **Address-first.** The primary conversion path is the plan-finder ("Where do
   you live?") and "Build your plan."
3. **Tile-grid merchandising.** Offers are rounded image hover-tiles in a grid.
4. **Brand chrome is constant.** Persistent mega-nav (Internet / Mobile / TV &
   Streaming / Home Security / Build your plan) and a deep multi-column footer.
5. **Editorial drives discovery.** /hub and /learn explainer content supports the
   merchandising with SEO long-form.
