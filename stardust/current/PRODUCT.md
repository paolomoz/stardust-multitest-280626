# PRODUCT — Samsung US (current state)

**Register:** brand (marketing/commerce landing surface)

## Users
- Consumers shopping for Galaxy mobile (phones, tablets, watches, buds, rings), TVs & audio, home appliances (refrigerators, laundry, dishwashers, vacuums), monitors, memory/storage, and accessories.
- Existing Samsung owners seeking support, warranty, downloads, repairs, registration, and account/order management.
- Prospective buyers researching features (AI Living, SmartThings, Galaxy AI) and editorial/how-to content (Explore).
- Business buyers (B2B entry point) and sustainability/about-us audiences (investors, press, careers).

## Product Purpose
Samsung US is the national commerce + brand site for Samsung Electronics America. It drives product discovery and direct purchase ("Buy Direct, Get More"), surfaces flagship innovation (Galaxy AI, AI Living, SmartThings), supports existing owners, and presents the corporate/sustainability identity. It is simultaneously a storefront, a brand showcase, and a support portal.

## Brand Personality
Confident, aspirational, and product-led with an accessible-premium tone. Big, bold display typography (SamsungSharpSans) over expansive product photography; clean white canvas; restrained color with a signature Samsung blue accent and black pill CTAs. Motion-forward heroes, large rounded cards, and a polished, editorial-meets-retail feel.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered spec-sheet retail pages with dense tables and loud promo badges.
- Generic SaaS template look; flat icon-only marketing.
- Heavy drop-shadow skeuomorphism — Samsung trends flat with surface-tint depth.

## Design Principles (observed)
1. **Imagery is the hero.** Product photography fills the frame; type and CTA float over it.
2. **Calm canvas, bold type.** White/`#f7f7f7` backgrounds let SamsungSharpSans headings carry hierarchy.
3. **One accent.** Samsung blue (`#2189ff`) for interactive emphasis; black for primary CTAs.
4. **Rounded, soft geometry.** 20px card radius, pill buttons, circular category chips.
5. **Dense but organized commerce.** Category tile rows → product card grids → detail; consistent card system across every category.
6. **Self-contained chrome.** A persistent GNB mega-nav and a deep footer tie the whole catalog + support + corporate surface together.
