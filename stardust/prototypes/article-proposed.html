How to Work From Home and Win the Day

Tips for working remotely without missing a beat.

Working from home can be a boon for productivity if it's done right. Whether you're joining the mobile workforce for the long haul or working from home temporarily, here are tips to make the most of your day.

Set the stage

Recreate your in-office set-up at home. Hook up your Galaxy S20 to a tool like Samsung DeX that lets you connect your phone to a monitor or TV with just a USB cable.

Find your rhythm

Good time management is crucial. Try breaking your day into productivity cycles: 15 minutes of work, then 5 minutes of break. Repeat that 4 times, then take a 20-minute rest. You can also write reminders on your Galaxy Note to stay on track.

Check in with your team

Send your boss a mid-day update on your progress. Even if you're not at your desk, you can use your phone as a webcam to video chat with your teammates, so you don't miss any conversations that affect your projects.

Boost your productivity

Make sure you've got the right equipment to excel. Get the power you need to work from anywhere with the Galaxy Book S and maximize precious desk space with the Space Monitor, which folds flat against the wall.

Keep moving

Don't feel tied to your desk. Move freely when you're on a call with wireless Galaxy Buds+. Two mics pick up your voice over background noise, so you'll be heard clear as a bell.

Make time to reset

Set some serious work-life boundaries to avoid burnout. Once your workday is over, settle in for an immersive gaming experience with a curved monitor — built for both work and fun.
