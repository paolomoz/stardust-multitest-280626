See what scams are trending and the red flags to be aware of to help protect yourself and your money.

Trending scams

Stopping scams is everyone's responsibility. Together, we can help keep your accounts protected.

Scammers pretend to be toll agencies

Don't click links in emails or reply to texts about an "unpaid toll."

Spot charity scams before you donate

Review quick tips to help protect your money.

Scammers prey on excited fans by selling fake tickets

Always use verified sites and watch out for offers that sound too good to be true.

Scam red flags

Scam details change, but red flags remain the same. Even if a story seems to make sense, verify the information through a trusted source.

A sense of urgency

Scammers pressure you to act immediately so you don't have time to think or verify.

A request for unusual payment

Be wary of requests to pay by gift card, wire transfer, cryptocurrency or payment app.

A request for personal information

Bank of America will never ask for your PIN, one-time passcode or full password.
