How to accept donations online

Gone are the days when people carried cash and checks regularly. From paying home expenses via a digital wallet to splitting a dinner bill with friends, people increasingly pay digitally.

Now think about this in terms of a nonprofit organization. If you don't make accepting donations just as easy, you could be missing out on contributions.

Read on to discover the benefits of accepting donations online as a nonprofit and learn about PayPal as a donation solution.

There are many benefits to accepting donations online as a nonprofit organization, from easy integration to building donor trust.
