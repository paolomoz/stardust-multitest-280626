# PRODUCT — Samsung US (redesign target)

**Register:** brand (commerce + brand showcase + support portal)

## Users
- **Shoppers** browsing Galaxy mobile (phones, tablets, watches, buds, rings), TVs & audio, home appliances (refrigerators, laundry, dishwashers, vacuums), monitors, memory/storage, and accessories — researching and buying direct.
- **Owners** seeking support: warranty, downloads/manuals, repairs, registration, contact, and account/order management.
- **Researchers** exploring innovation stories (Galaxy AI, AI Living, SmartThings) and editorial how-to content (Explore).
- **Corporate audiences** — sustainability, about-us, careers, investors, press.

## Product Purpose
Samsung US is the national storefront and brand home for Samsung Electronics America. It drives product discovery and direct purchase ("Buy Direct, Get More"), showcases flagship Galaxy innovation, supports existing owners end-to-end, and presents Samsung's corporate and sustainability identity — a storefront, a showcase, and a support portal in one.

## Brand Personality
Confident, aspirational, product-led, and accessibly premium. Big, bold display typography over expansive product photography on a calm white canvas. Restrained color with a single Samsung-blue accent and decisive black pill CTAs. Polished, editorial-meets-retail; motion-forward heroes; large soft-rounded cards. `product` · `modular-catalogue` · `transactional`.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered spec-sheet retail pages with dense tables and loud promo badges stacked edge to edge.
- Generic 2019–2021 SaaS template silhouette (centered hero + dual primary CTA in blue).
- Text baked into hero images (the current site's accessibility/SEO gap).
- Heavy drop-shadow skeuomorphism — Samsung trends flat with surface-tint depth.

## Design Principles
1. **Imagery leads, type anchors.** Product photography fills the frame; a real semantic headline and one CTA float over it — never text-in-image.
2. **Calm canvas, bold display type.** White / `#f7f7f7` grounds let SamsungSharpSans headlines carry hierarchy.
3. **One accent, one primary action.** Samsung blue `#2189ff` for interactive emphasis; a single black pill primary CTA per section, with a quiet "Learn more" link.
4. **Soft, rounded geometry.** 20px card radius, pill buttons, circular category chips; depth via surface tint, not shadow.
5. **Scannable catalog.** Category-tile row → responsive product-card grid → detail; a consistent card system on every category page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Promote in-image copy to real headings/paragraphs; maintain a legibility scrim where text overlays photography (AA contrast); preserve keyboard-navigable mega-nav and footer; reproduce hero motion with a `prefers-reduced-motion` static fallback.
