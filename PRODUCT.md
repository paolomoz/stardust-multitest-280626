# PayPal (US) — Target Strategy

## Register
**brand** — marketing/acquisition surface for paypal.com (US). Faithful
redesign, modernized: keep PayPal's identity, raise the execution to a
competent 2026 standard.

## Users
- **Consumers** — pay, send money, shop, earn cashback rewards, use
  buy-now-pay-later (Pay in 4 / Pay Monthly), manage a digital wallet,
  debit/credit cards.
- **Businesses / merchants** — accept payments (checkout, invoicing, virtual
  terminal, QR code), POS hardware, enterprise payment platforms.
- **Help-seekers** — existing users looking up how-to/FAQ articles and support.

## Product Purpose
Help people and businesses move money simply, safely, and everywhere.
The site acquires and educates two audiences and routes them to sign-up,
app download, product detail, the Business Resource Center, fees/pricing,
and self-service help.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, modern, optimistic, plain-spoken. Big confident typography,
generous whitespace, real lifestyle photography, bright blue accents on navy
and white. Security and privacy are a recurring, reassuring pillar — never
fear-based. Friendly without being frivolous; precise without being cold.

Traits (for motion-register selection): `product` + `transactional` +
`modular-catalogue` → motion register `kinetic-grid` (restrained,
grid-aware reveals; animated stat counters as the signature motion).

## Anti-references
- Not a dense fintech dashboard; not jargon-heavy.
- Not a dark, edgy crypto aesthetic.
- Not the generic-2026-SaaS silhouette (centered hero + dual primary-blue
  CTA + three feature cards) — explicitly guarded against.
- No legalese in primary copy; push terms to footnotes/legal hub.

## Design Principles
1. **Photography leads.** Real lifestyle/product imagery carries heroes and
   feature cards — never flat illustration or CSS scenery.
2. **Pill CTAs, one primary per view.** High-contrast blue-on-white or
   white-on-navy; a single dominant action, secondary as text-link.
3. **Trust woven in, not bolted on.** Security/buyer-protection as a calm
   recurring section, not an alarm.
4. **Modular, scannable sections.** Hero → feature trio → BNPL → rewards →
   security → send/receive. Scales from playful consumer pages to dense fee
   reference.
5. **Audience clarity.** Personal vs Business is the top-level IA split;
   never blur the two on a single page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Reserve bright accent blue (#0070E0) for sufficient-contrast
contexts; use deep navy (#001C64/#001435) for text. Legibility scrims under
any text-over-photo hero. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for stat counters.
