# PRODUCT — Starbucks (target)

**Register:** brand

## Users
US coffee drinkers and Starbucks® Rewards members browsing the menu, finding a store, ordering ahead, managing gift cards, and joining the loyalty program. Secondary audiences: job seekers, investors, business partners, press.

## Product Purpose
The warm, photography-led front door to Starbucks Coffee Company — showcase the seasonal lineup and full menu, make Rewards and gift cards effortless to join and use, and route every deeper action cleanly into ordering and account flows.

## Brand Personality
Warm, optimistic, premium-but-approachable. Coffee-craft heritage with a friendly contemporary voice ("It's a great day for coffee"). Calm, generous layouts; signature Siren green; large product photography.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered fast-food menu boards.
- Cold corporate SaaS hero (centered headline + dual blue CTA) — the Generic-2026-SaaS silhouette. Avoid.
- Discount/coupon-led promo design.

## Design Principles
- **Lead with the cup.** Real product/lifestyle photography carries every page; type supports, never substitutes.
- **One green that means action.** Siren green (#00754A) is reserved for primary CTAs; cream and deep-green bands add editorial warmth.
- **Generous and calm.** Balanced density (~64px section rhythm), large rounded cards, pill CTAs, clear one-hero-then-grid hierarchy.
- **Effortless routing.** Every page makes the next action (order, join, find a store) obvious and one tap away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA: green-on-white CTAs ≥ 4.5:1; legibility scrim under any text-over-photo hero; alt text on all product imagery; mixed-case headlines for readability.
