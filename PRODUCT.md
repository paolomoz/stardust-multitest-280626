# PRODUCT — Sony Group Portal (target)

## Register
**brand** — corporate marketing / investor / careers portal. Editorial, premium, institutional.

## Users
- Investors, analysts, shareholders (Investor Relations).
- Press and journalists (News Releases, corporate data).
- Prospective employees (Careers).
- General public, partners, and Sony enthusiasts (Products, Technology, Design, Sustainability).

## Product Purpose
The umbrella hub for Sony Group Corporation — presenting the group's businesses & products,
corporate information, technology programs, sustainability initiatives, design philosophy,
careers, and investor relations. A faithful, modernized refresh: keep Sony's monochrome
restraint and SST typography, sharpen hierarchy and editorial whitespace, let real
photography lead.

## Brand Personality
- Minimalist, precise, premium, confident.
- Photography- and story-led; imagery carries the page.
- Monochrome chrome (black/white) with generous whitespace.
- Culture-forward (film, music, audio, entertainment) yet corporate-serious.
- Editorial, slow-paced, publication-register.

## Anti-references
- Generic-2026-SaaS silhouette (centered hero + dual primary-blue CTAs).
- Cluttered corporate intranet; IR data tables as a homepage.
- Loud gradients, heavy drop-shadows, rounded "friendly" UI, glassmorphism.
- Stock-photo blandness; logo-as-editorial.

## Design Principles
1. **Photography first** — full-bleed imagery and a rotating story carousel lead every landing.
2. **Monochrome restraint** — black chrome, white canvas, grey hairlines; color comes from photos.
3. **SST voice** — Sony's typeface at display scale sets the entire identity.
4. **Editorial hierarchy** — true-black headings, muted-grey meta, generous whitespace.
5. **Quiet motion** — auto-rotating hero, subtle reveals; never gimmicky.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA contrast on all text; headings in true black, scrims behind hero text overlays.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` honored — carousel pauses, no autoplay.
- Real `alt` text on all imagery.
