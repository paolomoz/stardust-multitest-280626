# PRODUCT — Bank of America (target, redesign direction)

_Direction: faithful redesign, modernized. Mode A (brand-faithful), single variant._

## Register
**brand** — consumer-banking marketing site that sells/explains products and
provides a trusted, secure entry to Online Banking. Multi-audience IA.

## Users
- Consumer prospects and customers (checking, savings/CDs, credit cards, home & auto loans).
- Returning customers signing in to Online/Mobile Banking.
- Adjacent audiences routed via top nav: Small Business, Wealth/Merrill, Corporations.
- People seeking financial education (Better Money Habits) and security/fraud guidance.

## Product purpose
Help people choose financial products and manage their money with confidence,
and provide a safe, fast doorway to digital banking. Anchored to the brand line:
"What would you like the power to do?"

## Brand personality
Trustworthy, established, reassuring, civic, benefit-led. Big, safe institution
that foregrounds security (FDIC, fraud guidance) while staying approachable and
concrete ("6% cash back", "$200 online bonus offer"). Patriotic navy + red +
white flagscape identity. Traits: civic-formal, institutional, place-led.

## Anti-references
- No startup/hype tone, no playful slang, no dark patterns in disclosure.
- Avoid the Generic-2026-SaaS silhouette (centered hero + dual primary CTA + stock gradient).
- Not a sparse minimalist fintech aesthetic — this is a content-dense, multi-entry site.
- No logo-as-editorial, no fabricated rates/offers, no placeholder imagery.

## Design principles
- **Lead with the benefit.** Big numeral + plain-language offer first; detail second.
- **Show trust everywhere.** FDIC band, security links, equal-housing, real photography.
- **Card-grid clarity.** Every "choose your option" surface is a clean, scannable grid.
- **Real people, real products.** Authentic photography and accurate card renditions.
- **Modernized, recognisable.** Cleaner type scale, calmer spacing, refined cards — still unmistakably Bank of America.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Sufficient contrast on navy/blue bands (white text), focus states on
all CTAs, alt text on imagery, proper heading hierarchy (one h1/page).

## Motion
Register: **arrival** (civic-formal + institutional). Restrained entrance fades,
no gratuitous motion; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
