# Paramount — Target Product Spec

## Register
`brand` — corporate brand/marketing site for Paramount Skydance Corporation. Cinematic, entertainment-first.

## Product Purpose
Present Paramount as one of the world's leading producers of premium entertainment content. The site is the corporate face: brand portfolio, businesses, leadership & governance, history, news/press, careers, and investor pathways. **Scope:** faithful modernized redesign — same IA, same content beats, refreshed execution.

## Users
- Prospective employees & job applicants (careers, benefits, internships, life at Paramount)
- Press, media & investors (news, press releases, IR)
- Partners and the public researching the company and its brands

## Brand Personality
Bold, confident, cinematic. Oversized all-caps display headlines, deep-navy ink on white, a single Paramount-blue accent, full-bleed imagery and video heroes. Legacy + scale ("since 1912"). Entertainment, not tech-SaaS.

## Anti-references
- Generic 2026 SaaS landing silhouette (centered hero + dual primary-blue CTA + 3 feature cards). Forbidden.
- Dense data/dashboard chrome.
- Stock-photo scenery or text-only "CSS scenery" in place of real brand imagery.
- Logo-as-editorial (never use the wordmark as a content image).

## Design Principles
1. **Cinematic scale** — display type + full-bleed imagery/video carry every page.
2. **Portfolio is the hero** — the roster of brands is the primary content.
3. **Restrained palette, loud type** — navy + white + one blue accent; let the headlines shout.
4. **Real imagery only** — square brand cards, IP key art, leadership portraits; reproduce video heroes, never flatten.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast (navy on white passes comfortably; blue accent reserved for white grounds). Legibility scrim under any text over video/photo heroes. `prefers-reduced-motion` honored — video heroes fall back to poster stills.
