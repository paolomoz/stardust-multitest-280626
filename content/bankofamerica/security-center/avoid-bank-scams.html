Scam red flags

Scam details change, but red flags remain the same. Even if a story seems to make sense, verify the information through a trusted source.

A sense of urgency

Scammers pressure you to act immediately so you don't have time to think or verify.

A request for unusual payment

Be wary of requests to pay by gift card, wire transfer, cryptocurrency or payment app.

A request for personal information

Bank of America will never ask for your PIN, one-time passcode or full password.
